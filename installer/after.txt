To start using Outlook-Notes:

  1. Start Outlook (classic desktop).
  2. Click any mail.

The notepad docks automatically on the right, next to your mail. Its language
follows Outlook's own language automatically. You can also toggle it from the
"Outlook-Notes" group on the Home ribbon.

It works completely locally — no Microsoft login, no internet. Your notes and
screenshots are stored only on this computer under %APPDATA%\OutlookNotes.

If the notepad does not appear:
  * Make sure Outlook was fully closed during installation, then reopen it.
  * A log file at %APPDATA%\OutlookNotes\addin.log shows what happened.

To remove the add-in later, uninstall "Outlook-Notes" from Windows
Settings > Apps, then restart Outlook.
